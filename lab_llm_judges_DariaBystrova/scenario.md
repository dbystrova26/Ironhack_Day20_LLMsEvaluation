# Client Scenario — Step 1

A European retail bank wants to automate the generation of loan rejection letters to reduce manual workload and improve response time for declined applicants. Letters must be factually accurate (citing only verified rejection reasons), legally compliant with EU Consumer Credit Directive (CCD II), GDPR data minimisation principles, and EBA Guidelines on loan origination and monitoring, and empathetic in tone to preserve customer relationships and avoid reputational harm. The primary failure modes are: hallucination of rejection reasons not present in the applicant's profile (creating legal liability for inaccurate adverse action disclosure), omission of mandatory regulatory disclosures such as the applicant's right to request an explanation under CCD II Article 18 and their GDPR rights regarding automated decision-making (Article 22), and discriminatory language or differential tone across protected groups in violation of EU Equal Treatment Directives.
